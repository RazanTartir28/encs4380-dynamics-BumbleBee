Understanding τ

The time constant controls how quickly a first-order sensor responds.

At t = τ, a step response reaches 63.2% of its final value. At t = 3τ, it reaches approximately 95%.

1. Mathematical Model

First-order sensor model with zero initial conditions.

Differential Equation
τ dy/dt + y = Kx(t)
Transfer Function
G(s) = K/(τs+1)
Step Response
y(t) = K(1-e^(-t/τ))

2. Step Response

Response to a unit step input.

3. Logarithmic Error Analysis

The normalized transient error is E(t) = exp(-t/τ).

The vertical axis is logarithmic (base 10). The slope of ln(E) versus time is −1/τ. On the log10 graph, the slope is −1/(τ ln 10).
