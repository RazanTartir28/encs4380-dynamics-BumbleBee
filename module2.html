Understanding Frequency Response

A first-order sensor reduces the amplitude of higher-frequency signals and introduces phase lag.

At the cutoff frequency ωc = 1/τ, the amplitude ratio is approximately 0.707 (−3.01 dB), and the phase shift is −45°.

1. Frequency Response Overview

Steady-state response of a first-order sensor to sinusoidal input.
